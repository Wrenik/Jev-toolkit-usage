# Code of conduct

This is a small learning project. The rule is simple: be kind and be specific.

- Criticise code, ideas and results, not people. "This threshold looks wrong because ..." is welcome.
- Assume good faith; beginners and experts are both welcome here, and questions are not noise.
- No harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, or publishing someone else's private information.
- Keep issues on topic and free of secrets (API keys, private prompts, personal data).

The maintainer may edit, close or remove contributions that break these rules and may block repeat offenders.
To report a conduct problem, use GitHub's private reporting on the repository (Security tab, "Report a
vulnerability") and say it is a conduct report; do not include private details in a public issue.

This text is a short plain-language policy in the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
